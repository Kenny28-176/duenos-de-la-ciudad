City Owners

Buy streets, invest in the neighbourhood Stock Exchange and be the first to reach the goal.

  • The laptop or tablet is the board: it shows the city, moves the tokens, rolls the dice and acts as the bank.
  • Your phone is your wallet: your money, streets and shares, and the buttons to roll, buy, bid, build and trade.
  • Live market: every neighbourhood is listed on the Stock Exchange, and its price rises when someone builds there.

It is not playable yet, but you can already watch the board in a demo game between bots. You can also follow the development on GitHub (the rules draft is in Spanish).
